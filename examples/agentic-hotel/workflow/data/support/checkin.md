# Check-In and Stay Policy

This document covers arrival, check-in, identification, incidentals, room readiness, late arrival, check-out, and common stay-related situations. It is intended for SupportAgent answers and should be combined with booking details when a booking ID is available.

Support can explain these policies and look up bookings. In this demo, support cannot change arrival time, assign rooms, waive deposits, or contact the property directly.

## Standard Times

Standard check-in begins at 3:00 PM local hotel time. Standard check-out is 11:00 AM local hotel time.

These times are operational targets rather than absolute guarantees. A hotel may have rooms ready early, but housekeeping, maintenance, occupancy, and room type demand affect availability.

## Early Check-In

Early check-in means accessing the room before standard check-in time.

Common rules:

- Early check-in is subject to room availability.
- It is never guaranteed unless the guest books the prior night or buys a specific early-arrival package.
- A fee may apply, especially before noon.
- The requested room type may not be ready even if another room is available.

Example: A guest arrives at 9:00 AM after a red-eye flight. The hotel may store luggage and notify the guest when the room is ready. If the guest requires guaranteed access at 9:00 AM, the most reliable option is to book the previous night.

## Late Check-In and After-Midnight Arrival

A late arrival occurs when the guest arrives after normal evening check-in hours. An after-midnight arrival is still part of the original check-in date unless the hotel cancels the booking as a no-show.

Recommended guidance:

- Guests arriving late should notify the hotel as early as possible.
- A valid payment method may be required to hold the room overnight.
- If the guest arrives after the no-show cutoff without notice, the room may be released.
- For prepaid or guaranteed bookings, the hotel may hold the room longer, but this depends on property rules.

Example: A guest booked Monday night and arrives Tuesday at 1:30 AM. This is a late Monday arrival, not an early Tuesday check-in.

## Identification Requirements

At check-in, the hotel normally requires government-issued photo identification. The name on the ID should match the primary guest name on the booking.

Acceptable ID usually includes:

- Passport.
- Driver's license.
- National identity card.
- State or provincial ID.

Photos, photocopies, expired IDs, student IDs, or work badges may be rejected. International guests may need a passport even if they have other identification.

## Credit Card and Incidentals

Most hotels require a valid physical or digital payment card at check-in for incidentals, deposits, and possible damage charges.

Incidentals may cover:

- Room service.
- Parking.
- Minibar or marketplace purchases.
- Resort or destination fees.
- Damage, smoking fees, or missing items.
- Extra cleaning charges.

The incidentals hold is usually an authorization, not a completed charge. The unused portion is released after check-out, but the issuing bank controls how quickly the hold disappears.

## Third-Party Payer or Cardholder Not Present

If someone else paid for the booking, the hotel may still require the arriving guest to provide a card for incidentals. Some hotels require a credit card authorization form from the payer before arrival.

Support should explain that prepaid room charges and incidental authorization are different. A prepaid booking does not always remove the need for a card at check-in.

## Minimum Age

Hotels commonly require the primary guest to be at least 18 or 21 years old, depending on local law and property policy.

Important details:

- The age rule applies to the person checking in, not necessarily the person who paid.
- A minor may be refused check-in even with a valid booking.
- Some properties require a parent, guardian, or adult supervisor for youth sports teams or school groups.

Support should not guarantee that a minor can check in unless the property's age policy is known.

## Room Readiness and Room Type

The hotel tries to provide the booked room type, but individual room numbers, floor level, view, connecting rooms, and bed placement may be requests rather than guarantees.

Usually guaranteed:

- The confirmed hotel.
- The confirmed stay dates.
- The confirmed number of rooms.
- The booked room category, subject to availability and operational issues.

Usually requests:

- High floor.
- Quiet room.
- Room near elevator.
- Connecting rooms.
- Specific view.
- Early check-in.

If the booked room type is unavailable due to a hotel issue, the hotel may offer a comparable or upgraded room, a rate adjustment, or relocation assistance.

## Accessibility Accommodations

Guests may request accessible rooms or features such as roll-in showers, visual alarms, lower fixtures, or mobility-accessible paths.

Support should treat accessibility requests carefully:

- Do not describe accessibility as merely a preference.
- Explain that availability depends on room inventory and legal/property requirements.
- Encourage guests to contact the hotel directly for feature-specific confirmation.
- Do not promise a specific accessible feature unless the booking explicitly confirms it.

## Service Animals and Pets

Service animal rules differ from pet rules. A hotel may allow service animals even where pets are restricted, subject to applicable law.

Pet policy may include:

- Pet fee.
- Weight or breed restrictions.
- Limit on number of pets.
- Designated pet-friendly rooms.
- Cleaning or damage charges.

Support should not confuse emotional support animals, pets, and service animals because rules may differ by jurisdiction.

## Baggage Storage

Hotels commonly offer same-day baggage storage before check-in or after check-out, but it is usually subject to space and staffing.

Typical limits:

- Storage is for guests with same-day arrival or departure.
- Long-term storage may be refused.
- Valuables, cash, medication, passports, and fragile items should stay with the guest.
- The hotel may issue a claim ticket and require ID to retrieve bags.

## Parking and Arrival Logistics

Parking may be self-parking, valet, off-site, or unavailable. Parking charges are usually separate from room rates unless the booking explicitly includes parking.

Guests should verify:

- Parking fee.
- Vehicle height limits.
- In-and-out privileges.
- Electric vehicle charging.
- Oversized vehicle policy.
- Accessible parking options.

## Late Check-Out

Late check-out means remaining in the room after standard check-out time.

Common rules:

- Subject to availability.
- May be free for a short extension, such as noon instead of 11:00 AM.
- Half-day or full-day charges may apply for later departures.
- Housekeeping schedules and incoming reservations can prevent approval.

Example: A guest with a 7:00 PM flight asks for 5:00 PM check-out. The hotel may offer luggage storage instead of late check-out if the room is needed for an arriving guest.

## No-Smoking and Damage Rules

Many hotels are fully non-smoking. Smoking or vaping in the room may result in cleaning fees. Damage, missing items, excessive cleaning, or policy violations may be charged to the payment method on file.

Support should explain that these charges are property-assessed and may require direct review by hotel management.

## Example Scenarios

### Guest Arrives Before Noon

The hotel can store luggage and check room readiness, but room access is not guaranteed. A fee may apply if early check-in is available.

### Guest Has No Card for Incidentals

The hotel may refuse check-in or require a cash deposit if allowed. A prepaid booking does not always remove the incidental requirement.

### Guest Arrives After Midnight

The arrival is still tied to the original check-in date. The guest should notify the hotel to avoid no-show handling.

### Guest Requests Connecting Rooms

The hotel can note the request, but connecting rooms are usually not guaranteed unless the booking specifically confirms them.

### Guest Needs Accessible Shower

Support should advise confirming the exact accessible feature with the hotel. General room availability does not guarantee every accessibility feature.
